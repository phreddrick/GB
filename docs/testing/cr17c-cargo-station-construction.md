# CR-17c atomic Cargo Station construction

[Issue #44](https://github.com/phreddrick/GB/issues/44) tracks the ordinary star-orbit Cargo Ship path for `build S`, `build S 1` and `build S 2`. This slice covers those command forms with autoload enabled or disabled. Other builders, orbits, ship types, God builds and command forms keep their existing routing. The detached-state coverage for [#43](https://github.com/phreddrick/GB/issues/43) uses the legacy producer where it needs the historical split save. Its consumers still run through the dispatcher.

On the pre-change server, real dispatch of `build S 2` on Cargo #77 reported success for each child before writing its row. After the command and a reopen without a shutdown flush, Station rows #78 and #79 remained in bbolt while the star head was still #77 and the stored builder still held its original resources.

The actor witnesses the entire star exterior and compares it to the independently walked durable chain. A valid pending difference or unavailable ID defers without writing. A broken structural witness halts. The typed store entry point recalculates all Station rows and the builder debit from the witnesses, compares exact preimages, and rechecks each ID for absence and structural claims. One bbolt transaction stores the affordable prefix, the Cargo balance and load debits, the resulting star head, and the consumed free-list entries. Free entries are popped in LIFO order. Fresh IDs append above the greatest number present or already allocated in the command. A request for two that can fund only one consumes only one ID. A request that cannot fund one makes no writes.

Scope, technology, designs, damage, placement, prepend order, AP, builder mass and autoload semantics stay compatible with the legacy path. The actor installs every committed row and the final list before the first success notice. It reports builds in allocation order and follows the last build's notices with any remaining affordability diagnostic. Late conflicts and uncertain commits halt without a legacy retry or shutdown flush. A confirmed durable commit with an installation or reporting failure also requires reconciliation. Live notices can be lost if the process stops after commit.

The dispatcher tests cover all three forms, both autoload settings, zero and partial affordability, LIFO reuse and mixed allocation, durable and actor state at first output, broken and pending witnesses, the CR-18 write fence, late maintenance, and late record or allocator changes. Store contract tests reject forged IDs, tickets, placement, cost and incomplete prefixes. The process tests kill the test server with SIGKILL before and between writes, at the last precommit hook, after commit and on both sides of actor installation. The first comparison uses a fresh bbolt open and checks the whole logical image against the expected old or new state. Mixed and partial builds also pass scheduled segment, update, restart and numeric dock/undock checks after postcommit process death. These tests exercise process failure at the specified boundaries. Physical I/O failure and power loss remain outside their coverage.
