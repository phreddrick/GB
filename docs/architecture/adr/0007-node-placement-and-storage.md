# ADR-0007: Node placement, Longhorn persistence and the single-writer volume (ReadWriteOncePod with a declared fallback)

Status: Proposed 2026-10-30 (Phase 2B draft for owner review). Cluster re-inspected read-only 2026-11-01 (Phase 2D Checkpoint 1): the versions, StorageClass, CSIDriver policy, node labels and settings below were unchanged and no RWOP volume was present in the inventory that day. **Storage decision taken 2026-11-03: RWOP CONFIRMED for the tested RKE2 / Longhorn configuration; `ReadWriteOncePod` is retained in the `gb-dev` StatefulSet and the RWO fallback of decision 4 is not activated.** The owner approved this conclusion after the disposable storage validation of `docs/operations/storage-and-network-validation.md` section 1 completed through P11 on 2026-11-03 (optional P7 skipped): Longhorn v1.12.1 provisioned a 1Gi `ReadWriteOncePod` claim whose PV and Longhorn volume both reported RWOP; a same-node second Pod was refused by the scheduler (`PersistentVolumeClaim with ReadWriteOncePod access mode already in-use by another pod`) and never started; a Pod running as UID/GID 65532 with `fsGroup: 65532` found the ext4 mount `0:65532` mode `2775` and wrote the marker without any init container or `chown`; after the writer's deletion the VolumeAttachment disappeared and Longhorn reported `detached` within 17 s, and a replacement writer read the marker back with the exact SHA-256 (`1f9cdf60a2089c9a15b7fd40469f9a8c39e920160f74281fdd34507a118e9921`) before any write; the claim, PV, Longhorn volume and namespace were then reclaimed through their controllers with no residue. Record: `docs/testing/phase2-validation.md` sections 2 to 4 and 9 (operator-held evidence archive `rwop-evidence-2026-11-03`). The result covers single-writer exclusion, the non-root mount and orderly handoff on this cluster only; it does not cover crash or kill mid-flush (CR-18, decision 8), node partitions, or application-level corruption, and it is not deployment authorisation (gates B and C of the validation record stay OPEN). Decisions 1, 2 and 5 to 8 and the rest of this ADR are unchanged; the ADR's overall status remains Proposed pending the owner's review of the deployment.

## Context

Verified read-only on 2026-10-30: Longhorn v1.12.1 with CSI provisioner v5.3.0 and attacher v4.12.0, `CSIDriver` `fsGroupPolicy: ReadWriteOnceWithFSType`, StorageClass `longhorn` (reclaim `Delete`, `Immediate` binding, 2 replicas, ext4, daily backup recurring job), `node-down-pod-deletion-policy: do-nothing`, `auto-salvage: true`; every PVC and PV present that day was `ReadWriteOnce` and no RWOP volume was present in that inventory (whether one ever existed is not knowable from an inventory). Longhorn added RWOP support in v1.11.0. Kubernetes 1.36 (RWOP GA since 1.29). All 11 nodes are Ready and **none is tainted**, control-plane nodes included. `workload.home-apps: "true"` is on `backup` (4 CPU, 15 GiB, nearly idle) and `wkr1` (8 CPU, 7.7 GiB, about half its memory in use); `wkr4` is the GPU node; `wkr3` has 92 % of its memory committed in limits. bbolt tolerates exactly one writer; CR-18 (non-atomic turn flush) is open until Phase 3a.

Re-verified read-only on 2026-11-01 (Phase 2D Checkpoint 1, kubectl context `local` through the Rancher proxy, GET/LIST only): Longhorn v1.12.1 (Helm revision 10), csi-attacher v4.12.0, csi-provisioner v5.3.0, csi-resizer v2.2.1, csi-snapshotter v8.6.0, all 3/3; `CSIDriver driver.longhorn.io` still `fsGroupPolicy: ReadWriteOnceWithFSType`, `attachRequired: true`; StorageClass `longhorn` unchanged (`Delete`, `Immediate`, 2 replicas, ext4, not the default class); all 11 Longhorn nodes Ready and Schedulable; 12 Longhorn volumes, all `rwo`, none RWOP, none referencing GB; `backup` (393 GiB free) and `wkr1` (367 GiB free) still the only `workload.home-apps` nodes, both Ready, untainted, no pressure. Version and manifest facts are not behaviour: nothing above proves that a second pod is refused the volume, that the attachment moves, or that the mount is group-writable by UID 65532.

## Decision

1. **Placement by the convention label, chosen by the scheduler.** `nodeSelector: workload.home-apps: "true"`, requests 100m/256Mi, limits 1 CPU/1Gi, no affinity, no hostname pin, no tolerations. The selector is mandatory on this cluster: without it a pod may land on a control-plane node. The scheduler's least-allocated scoring makes `backup` the expected home; `wkr1` remains eligible, which the drain acceptance test needs. Extending the label to other workers is a `k8s-helpers` change.
2. **Storage identity frozen with the StatefulSet** (ADR-0005, plan 12.3 item 5): `volumeClaimTemplates[0]` is `name: world`, `storageClassName: longhorn`, `accessModes: [ReadWriteOncePod]`, `5Gi`; `persistentVolumeClaimRetentionPolicy: {whenDeleted: Retain, whenScaled: Retain}`; `podManagementPolicy: OrderedReady`; `replicas: 1`. The PVC is therefore `world-gb-engine-0`, mounted at `/data/world`, with `GB_DB_PATH=/data/world/gb.db`.
3. **RWOP is the design, validated on a disposable claim; no `gb-dev` claim exists yet.** Before the first `gb-dev` PVC exists, the authorised throwaway test in `docs/operations/storage-and-network-validation.md` must show: a second pod is refused the volume cluster-wide, the attachment moves when the first pod is deleted, and the mount is group-writable by 65532 through `fsGroup` without an init container. **Shown on 2026-11-03** (same-node refusal at the scheduler; detachment and handoff with byte-exact persistence; non-root write), recorded in the status line above and in `docs/testing/phase2-validation.md`. The frozen access mode stays `ReadWriteOncePod`.
4. **Declared fallback (not activated: RWOP confirmed 2026-11-03).** If RWOP had failed on this cluster, the frozen access mode would have become `ReadWriteOnce` **before any PVC is created** (it is immutable afterwards). RWO is weaker: it is enforced per node, so a second pod on the same node could mount the volume. Single-writer semantics then rest on the StatefulSet (one pod per ordinal, `OrderedReady`, never a Deployment), `replicas: 1` enforced by the validator, bbolt's exclusive file lock (5 s timeout, exit 1), and the rule that nothing else ever references `world-gb-engine-0`. The gap is recorded; the advisory owner lease of plan 11.3 arrives in Phase 3a.
5. **Non-root writes without privilege.** `fsGroup: 65532` with `fsGroupChangePolicy: OnRootMismatch` relies on Longhorn's `ReadWriteOnceWithFSType` policy to make the ext4 mount group-writable; the process (UID 65532) creates the database file itself. No `chown`, no init container, no `hostPath`.
6. **Reclaim and protection.** The `longhorn` class deletes the volume with the PVC; the retention policy protects the PVC from StatefulSet deletion and scaling; `k8s-helpers` rules (no finalizer, non-cascading Application delete) protect it from Argo CD. Nothing protects against `kubectl delete pvc`, which is therefore the explicit, owner-authorised step of the regeneration procedure (ADR-0008). A `Retain` class or PV patch is a production concern.
7. **Node loss stays manual.** `do-nothing` is kept (plan Decision 30): a failed node leaves the pod `Terminating` and the volume attached until an operator confirms the node is down and force-deletes the pod (`docs/operations/deploy.md`, node-loss section). Acceptable for disposable development data.
8. **Persistence claim, stated exactly.** Longhorn replicates blocks synchronously; an orderly shutdown (exit 0) leaves a consistent file that survives restarts and node moves. A crash or kill mid-flush leaves a mixed-turn database on every replica alike (CR-18). Replication does not make the multi-transaction flush atomic and the daily backup job copies whatever state exists. `gb-dev` data is disposable until Phase 3a.

## Alternatives considered

- `nfs` (the cluster default class): rejected for a bbolt file (locking and fsync semantics).
- `longhorn-ollama` or a new `longhorn-retain` class: `Retain` is desirable for prod, not needed for a disposable dev volume; a new class is a `k8s-helpers` change deferred to Phase 8.
- Pinning to `backup` by hostname: rejected; it defeats the drain test and the convention.
- WaitForFirstConsumer binding: the `longhorn` class is `Immediate` and data locality is disabled, so binding before scheduling costs nothing here.
- Running as root with a `chown` init container: rejected (plan 11.6, PSS `restricted`).

## Consequences

- The access mode decision is a one-way door per PVC; the runbook sequence puts the RWOP test before the first server sync for that reason.
- A second universe is a second StatefulSet (plan 4.1 item 10), not a second replica; multi-universe naming is deferred (Phase 2B Decision D).
- Memory limits of 1Gi are a starting value; the plan's 200m/2 CPU engine numbers apply after measurement in Phase 3a.
